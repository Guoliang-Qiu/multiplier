Analyze the parent four-input multiplier's static Circuit DAG and perform one
small, local rewrite of a logic region. The circuit computes `a*b + c*d` for
four independent 9-bit two's-complement Q2.7 inputs `a`, `b`, `c`, and `d`, and
exposes a 19-bit two's-complement Q4.14 output. Prefer a rewrite that reduces
output-reachable gate cost without needlessly discarding useful parent
structure. Preserve reusable subgraphs when possible, and consider combining
compatible subgraphs from the two product paths if that is locally safe.
Preserve the zero-argument `build_circuit()` interface, fixed `a`, `b`, `c`, `d`
bus order, and the shared DAG format. Do not add simulation, scoring, or
custom gate-cost code. The approximation must be part of the runnable circuit,
not a post-processing rule. Evaluate the resulting implementation with the
existing `eval.py` procedure and use its reported `evm_db` together with the
weighted gate cost to judge the rewrite. After the rewrite, verify that the
implementation can be imported and produces a valid DAG.
