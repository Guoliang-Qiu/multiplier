The baseline already has 24 MUX_XOR cells mapping two Booth digit groups, whose sharing makes additional cells nontrivial. Screened extension of the same Booth-digit identity to step 3 (36 cells, cost 993.8; full-stimulus raw equality 0 mismatches), a different equivalent negative selector factoring (cost 999.8), retaining cells in only step 1 (cost 972.6), only step 2 (975.6), or only low/high three bit positions (978.6 and 985.6). None beat the 968.2 general baseline; retained it.
