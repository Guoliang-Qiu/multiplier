General pass inspected all three stages: Booth-product selectors, step-four correction and insertion, constant-bearing weighted columns, custom low-column majority compression, exact upper compression and custom output carry handling. Tap2 handles constant folding and hash-consing, so these were not reimplemented. A common-factor rewrite of `(shared & left) | (shared & right)` and its dual (limited to `_gate`) removed four reachable AND gates while preserving all 94 pre-compression slots. The original absorption override remains genuinely necessary: disabling it raises cost to 964.2, even with factoring. Removing the specialized `_and` override increased cost to 970.2; replacing custom XOR construction with native XOR raised cost to 983.2. Compressor and output `_add3` argument permutations had no savings; replacing the step-four sign selector with its explicit positive/negative minterms raised cost to 992.2. A De Morgan and complemented absorption pass produced no savings. Raw handoff/general-baseline outputs were bitwise equal on all 1,050,624 configured stimuli (empirical, not formal equivalence).

Directional pass inspected installed tap2 `MUX_XOR` truth table and port order `(a,b,c,d,e)`: `e ^ ((a & b) | (c & d))`; weighted cost 4.8, versus AND/OR=1, XOR=3 and NOT=0. Existing 24 MUX_XOR cells remain intact. Additional step-three Booth MUX_XOR cells raised whole-circuit cost to 983.8; step-one-only and step-two-only mappings raised cost to 966.6 and 969.6, respectively. Full-adder MUX_XOR parity mapping raised cost to 1081.2 and signed-digit MUX_XOR mapping to 1013.4. No extra cell placement improved weighted cost. The surrounding insertion-buffer simplification replaced an unshared `OR(d[0],d[1])` followed by a raw `OR(...,ZERO)` with tap2's cached `_or(d[0],d[1])`, removing one reachable OR without changing the column slot. This is the only directional-pass improvement; no extra MUX_XOR placement is retained. Handoff/final raw output was bitwise identical over every configured stimulus case (empirical, not a formal proof). Final weighted savings: 5.0 versus handoff and 10.0 versus original parent; final trades 0.0585 dB EVM relative to parent and meets the strict -57.6 dB threshold. The final is a Pareto trade-off versus the original parent: lower cost, same slot count, worse EVM. Parent and handoff snapshots were not modified.
