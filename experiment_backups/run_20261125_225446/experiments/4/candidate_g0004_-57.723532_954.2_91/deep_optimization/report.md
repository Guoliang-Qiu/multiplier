## General equivalence pass (completed before directional search)

Measured exact-adder swaps for the compressor globally and individually by column, factored-versus-reference final ripple adder, alternative XOR decompositions, MUX_XOR Booth-row coverage including step 3 and sign bits, global absorption on/off, and repeated Boolean-cleanup passes. The shared tap2 constant folding and absorption already cover the useful trivial gate rewrites. This is sample-based equality, not a formal proof. The rewritten buffers are elementary Boolean identities; the indirect effects of changing dispatch were checked by that full raw comparison. No truth-table synthesis service was available in the configured local tool API, so no SAT/SMT results are claimed.

## Directional pass (starting from the saved general baseline)

Tested individual constant correction removals, G5 omission, output-unreachable bit-18 correction removal, deletion of the zero G6 slot, and a weighted-cost screen for individual column-term deletions (all temporary trials under this directory). The stimulus consists of 1,050,624 four-input signed samples; high-weight corrections exhibit an overwhelming signed bias when removed: omitting bit 10 yields EVM -29.5734 dB, bit 11 -23.5633 dB, bit 13 -11.5158 dB, bit 15 +0.6843 dB. The seemingly redundant G6 zero participates in the approximate compressor's structural dispatch, so removing it degrades EVM to -55.4854 dB despite lower cost. Removing compensation G5 leads to -56.5466 dB. These failures were rejected against strict -57.6 dB; no local error estimate was treated as sufficient. Higher-column bit 18 is different: `_build_outputs` explicitly sets output 18 to output 17, making its correction unobservable and leaving every measured error metric unchanged. Removing only that slot gives [954.2, -57.72353155447783, 91], improves the original parent and two archive records, and has no strict archive dominator. The unchanged output-reachable logic explains the identical cost and EVM, and a separate full-stimulus raw-output bitwise comparison confirmed **zero mismatches** between handoff and the 91-slot trial. This is sample-based verification, not a formal proof. MUX_XOR is retained with its verified `(a,b,c,d,e)` order and fractional 4.8 cost.
