## Ordered pass 1: general equivalence

Inspected product generation, signed high Booth cells, the custom XOR and AND selector sharing, majority compressor schedules, `G` correction, output carry and truncation, and installed tap2 `_fa`, `_ha`, `_add3`, `_gate`. Replaced fragile handoff numeric gate IDs (94, 165, 417) with explicitly captured column-five slots, removing dead G locals; `equivalent.py` has 0 raw-output mismatches versus handoff on **all 1,050,624 supplied examples**, weighted cost identical at 970.2 and EVM identical. This is sample-based equivalence, not a formal proof. Other equal-output trials: native XOR costs 989.2; original high-sign Booth SOP costs 998.2; disabling global absorption changes nothing; removing selector factoring costs 976.2. A native parity rewriting of G does **not** preserve handoff's approximate G result (39/2102 sampled mismatches), and unsorting compressors changes outputs (307/1051 sampled mismatches); therefore neither is part of the equivalence baseline. Exhaustively screened 64 subsets of sorted columns 4–9; no strictly cheaper sampled-equivalent schedule was found. Specialized exact-synthesis tool was not available in this API session; no SAT proof is claimed.

## Ordered pass 2: directional trials

The handoff folds three original column-five partial products into two sum/carry precompression slots, concealing an arithmetic compressor inside partial-product generation. For the stage-correct directional design, `_product_columns` preserves all three original weighted terms and `_compress_columns` uses inherited tap2 `_fa` to compress these terms *before* the usual custom approximate tree. The cost-neutral `staged_g.py` circuit exactly matches the parent raw output on the entire supplied stimulus (0 mismatches), costs 967.2, EVM -57.83461265306508, and honestly counts 93 slots. The final circuit additionally replaces column-four slot 1 with ONE. It costs 955.2, counts 93 slots, and meets the strict -57.6 dB limit. This is a 12.0 weighted-cost reduction relative to the immutable parent, at a measured EVM penalty of 0.15927313 dB. It is a valid cost/accuracy tradeoff relative to its parent, **but is dominated by the existing archive point [954.2, -57.72353155447783, 92]**; it must not be represented as a cross-generation Pareto improvement.

Distribution-bias screenings used only subsamples of the required external stimulus, with full evaluation of promising trials. Handoff-style unsorted compression failed at EVM -54.89303190: changes in the approximate majority scheduling produce widespread rounding errors. Removing the explicit ZERO column-four slot reduced cost to 960.2 and slots to 91 but failed at -55.34720747: changing compressor scheduling makes the rounding errors much larger (RMSE 0.41236). Exact G parity costs 972.2, EVM -57.83461265; native `_fa` plus removal of two artificial OR(x,ZERO) buffers costs 967.2, EVM -57.83461265 and yields the stage-correct parent-equivalent implementation. Selective rounding OR thresholds above column 7 caused large EVM regressions in screening. Removing any of signed correction constants at columns 10, 11, 13, 15 gives severe systematic error; removing the column-four compensator failed the local screen. Swapping slot 1 for a reused column-four signal or deleting it also failed or degraded accuracy: the fully evaluated deletion produced EVM -56.08770460 with 92 slots. The selected constant slot-one circuit had full EVM -57.67533952, MAE 0.26609180, RMSE 0.31540805, max error 0.875. Reusing a nearby column-four input gave identical full outputs, cost, and slots, so it was not retained. Existing MUX_XOR gates were kept in their verified installed `(a,b,c,d,e)` orientation at cost 4.8; no primitive-only expansion or gate-cost override was used.

The delivered source is standalone with only the tap2 library project import and a parameterless `build_circuit()`. Stage boundaries remain honest: product terms in `_product_columns`, joint full-adder G compression and approximate tree in `_compress_columns`, final carry and 19-bit output construction in `_build_outputs`.
