# Deep optimization record

## General equivalence pass (completed before directional search)

- Immutable original parent and handoff are byte-for-byte the same: cost **968.2**, EVM **-57.83461265306508 dB**, pre-compression slots **94**. Full workspace `gate_cost.py` and `eval.py --stimulus-file /home/guoliang/multiplier/stimulus.npy` both completed on each snapshot.
- Traced all 19 weighted columns before/after compression and inspected the tap2 primitives, full adder, cache, and gate costs. Probed standard XOR, several equivalent SOP/XNOR XOR realizations, native and alternative shared full adders, sign-digit SOP versus XOR factoring, and several compressor sorting schedules. None reduced weighted reachable cost. The full-adder XOR rewrite and sorting variants were checked for raw-output equality on the configured stimulus; these are **sample-based checks, not formal proofs**.
- Removed the unused ONE at column 18: `_build_outputs` explicitly discards the computed bit 18 and repeats bit 17 as the sign output. This removes one honest pre-compression slot without altering any reachable output. Also extended the existing carry-only compressor selection to column 4; all its outputs below bit 6 are forced to ZERO, and column-4 carry remains unchanged. This saves one reachable AND/OR pair. Sample-based bitwise raw equality on all 1,050,624 configured stimulus cases confirmed.
- Saved the equivalently optimized implementation as `general_equivalence_baseline.py`. Its complete measured objectives: **[967.2, -57.83461265306508 dB, 93]**. No exact-synthesis tool was called: no small fully specified cone appeared compelling relative to low-cost tap2 sharing, and exact synthesis was not necessary to establish either of the chosen rewrites.

## Directional search (after saving the general baseline)

- Distribution diagnosis used the external stimulus only. Baseline raw error mean was -36.5209 counts; errors were nearly identical under signs of the sampled multipliers and when the two products cancel versus reinforce. The evaluator stimulus is triangular in multiplier signs (`b>=0,d<0` has no cases). Statistical bias guided one-bit compensation trials; no post-processing was fitted.
- Honest column-slot removal, several compensation ONE removals, carry-only approximate compressors across columns 4–17, and extra low-column rounding were tested by complete-circuit `gate_cost.py` and (for promising cases) `eval.py`. Column-4 ZERO removal reduced cost to 967.2 and slots to 93 but degraded EVM to -55.28144 dB; removal of correction at column 10/13/15 gave drastic errors. Additional column-5 carry-only compression yielded cost 944.2 but EVM -54.72468 dB. An additional ONE at column 6 gave EVM -52.73187 dB. All are ineligible under the strict -57.6 dB threshold.
- Deleting a second column-4 product slot with explicit +16 compensation produced several feasible variants. Strongest inexpensive variant (`drop1_comp16_nozero.py`) measured **[955.2, -57.67533952310306 dB, 93]**, a cost/error Pareto trade-off against the supplied parent; another (`drop2_comp16.py`) measured **[951.2, -57.60544201249748 dB, 94]**, just below the EVM limit. Both are already dominated by the current archive entry **[954.2, -57.72353155447783 dB, 92]**. This is why the equivalence-preserving baseline was selected as the delivered candidate instead of a strictly worse cross-generation trade-off.
- A mixed MUX_XOR realization of the sign selector cost 1020.4 (instead of 967.2); replacing the existing MUX_XOR with ordinary gates was not justified. The library MUX_XOR remains intact with supported fractional cost 4.8.

## Delivery

The selected candidate is `general_equivalence_baseline.py`: cost **967.2**, EVM **-57.83461265306508 dB**, honest partial-product count **93**. Its feasible Pareto trade-off against the original parent is strictly lower cost and fewer slots at equal EVM. Formal all-input proof was not claimed; bitwise raw equality was checked on the configured external stimulus. The candidate remains a standalone readable three-stage `Candidate(MultiplierCircuit)` using shared tap2.
